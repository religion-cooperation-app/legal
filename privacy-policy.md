# Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 2026-01-16  
**Last updated:** 2026-01-16

## 1. Who We Are

This application (the “App”) is operated by the **Experimental Anthropology Lab**, a research group affiliated with the **University of Connecticut** (“we,” “us,” or “our”).

The App is used exclusively for **academic research purposes** and is not a commercial product.

**Contact:**  
For privacy-related questions or data requests, contact:  
📧 **rc.research.app.info@gmail.com**

---

## 2. Scope of This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains:

- What information we collect through the App
- How that information is used and stored
- The legal basis for processing personal data
- Users’ rights, including rights under the GDPR

Details about **study procedures, risks, and participation** are provided separately in the **Research Consent Form**, which users must review and accept before participating.

---

## 3. Information We Collect

Depending on study configuration and user participation, the App may collect the following categories of data:

### a. Information You Provide Directly
- Survey responses
- Study-related reflections or task responses
- Game or activity decisions

### b. Automatically Collected Research Data
- **Geolocation data**, including entry into predefined public geofences
- **Bluetooth proximity data**, used to measure device-to-device proximity
- Timestamps and activity completion metadata

### c. Technical and Analytics Data
- App usage events
- Error logs and performance metrics
- Interaction metadata used for internal research analytics

The App does **not** collect:
- Advertising identifiers
- Commercial profiling data
- Contact lists or personal media
- Payment or financial information

---

## 4. Sensitive Technologies Disclosure

The App may use background sensing technologies, including:

- Background location services
- Bluetooth-based proximity sensing

These technologies are:
- Used **only** when required by the research protocol
- Limited to predefined public locations or study conditions
- Fully disclosed during the consent process

---

## 5. How We Use Information

We use collected information exclusively for:

- Conducting academic research
- Analyzing behavioral and social patterns
- Improving study design and app functionality
- Ensuring data integrity and security

We do **not**:
- Sell personal data
- Use data for advertising or marketing
- Share identifiable data with third parties

---

## 6. Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR)

For users located in the European Union, data processing is based on:

- **Explicit consent** (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR)
- **Scientific research purposes** with appropriate safeguards (Article 89 GDPR)

Consent is obtained during onboarding and may be withdrawn at any time.

---

## 7. Data Storage and Security

- Data is stored using **Google Firestore** and related Google Cloud infrastructure
- Access is restricted to authorized members of the research team
- Identifying information is stored separately from research and sensor data
- Reasonable technical and organizational safeguards are in place

---

## 8. Data Retention

- Withdrawing from the study **stops further data collection**
- Previously collected data is generally retained for research integrity and analysis
- Participants may request **full deletion** of their data by contacting us

Deletion requests will be honored unless retention is required by ethical, legal, or academic integrity obligations, in which case this will be explained.

---

## 9. Data Sharing

Data may be:
- Aggregated
- De-identified
- Anonymized

before being shared in:
- Academic publications
- Conference presentations
- Open scientific datasets

No shared data will reasonably identify individual participants.

---

## 10. International Data Transfers

Data may be processed or stored in the **United States** or other jurisdictions where cloud infrastructure is located.

Appropriate safeguards are applied in accordance with applicable data-protection laws.

---

## 11. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

- Access your personal data
- Correct inaccurate data
- Request deletion
- Restrict or object to processing
- Withdraw consent
- Lodge a complaint with a data-protection authority

Requests can be made by emailing **rc.research.app.info@gmail.com**.

---

## 12. Children

This App is **not intended for individuals under 18 years of age**.  
We do not knowingly collect data from minors.

---

## 13. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.  
The effective date will be revised accordingly.
