# Data Deletion Request

We respect your right to control your personal data.

## How to Request Deletion

You can request deletion of your account and associated data in one of the following ways:

1. **In-App Request**:
   Navigate to Profile Page → Settings → Delete Account and Data

2. **Email Request**:
   Send an email to rc.research.app.info@gmail.com

   Please include:
   - The email associated with your account
   - Your app username

## What Data Will Be Deleted

Upon verification, we will delete:

- All Account information and data, including
- (email, UID)
- Survey responses
- Location data (including geolocation history)
- Social network data
- Any associated study participation records

## Data Retention

- Data will be deleted within **30 days** of request

## Data Deletion Request Period

Data Deletion requests are only possible within the study period described in the research consent form that users sign upon enrolling in the App, and can access at any time within the app via Profile Page → Settings → Delete Account and Data. Following closure of the study period, all data is anonymized and there is no way to associate users with their data.

## Verification

We may request additional information to verify your identity before processing deletion.
