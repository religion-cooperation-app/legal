# SPARRC Account & Data Deletion Request

This page describes how users of the **SPARRC** mobile application ("SPARRC") can request deletion of their account and associated data.

SPARRC is operated by University of Connecticut Experimental Anthropology Lab for academic research and data collection purposes.

We respect your right to control your personal data.


---

## How to Request Deletion

You may request deletion of your SPARRC account and associated data using one of the following methods:

### 1. In-App Request

Open the SPARRC application and navigate to:

**Profile Page → Settings → Delete Account and Data**

### 2. Email Request

Send a deletion request to:

**rc.research.app.info@gmail.com**

Please include:
- The email associated with your account
- Your SPARRC username

---

## What Data Will Be Deleted

Upon verification of your identity, we will delete:

- Account information and identifiers (including email address and UID)
- Survey responses
- Location and geolocation history
- Social network and proximity interaction data
- Study participation records associated with your account

---

## Data Retention

Deletion requests are processed within **30 days** of verification.

---

## Study Period & Data Anonymization

Data deletion requests are available only during the active study participation period described in the participant consent form provided during enrollment in the SPARRC application.

After the study period concludes, identifying account information is permanently removed and research data is anonymized such that it can no longer be associated with individual users.

---

## Verification

We may request additional information to verify account ownership before processing deletion requests.
